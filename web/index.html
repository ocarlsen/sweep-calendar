Sweep Calendar

Street-sweeping calendars for your city.

How it works

  1. Search your address, or zoom the map to your block.
  2. Tap the line on your side of the street. Each side sweeps on its own schedule.
  3. Subscribe. Your calendar shows every no-parking window and updates itself, holidays included.

    Posted signs control. Schedules come from city open data and can lag sign changes. Check the curb.

    Anonymous visit counts via GoatCounter: no cookies, and no address or location is ever sent.
